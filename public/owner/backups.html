Backups

Protect your data against computer loss, accidental deletion, or disk failure

Manual Backup

Downloads a single .zip file to your computer right now, containing: a full database snapshot, a ready-to-open customers.xlsx and orders.xlsx (real Excel files, no app needed to view them), workers_directory.csv and attendance_log.csv for employee data, gold_receipts.csv and maker_commissions.csv for the gold ledgers, and every photo/document ever uploaded. Save this somewhere safe — like a USB drive, Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

Automatic Backups

While this app's server is running, it automatically creates a backup .zip roughly once a week and stores it on this computer (in app/data/backups/), keeping the most recent 8 automatically and deleting older ones.

Important — what this does and doesn't protect against Automatic backups are saved on this same computer, so they won't help if this computer is lost, stolen, or its hard drive fails. For real protection, periodically click "Backup Now" above and copy the downloaded file to a USB drive, cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox), or email it to yourself or a trusted person. Treat the automatic backups as a convenience for quick recovery from accidental deletions — not a substitute for an off-site copy.
