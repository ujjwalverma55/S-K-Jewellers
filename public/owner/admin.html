Maker Gold Commission Rate

What percentage of a delivered order's weight is paid to the assigned Maker as gold commission. For example, a rate of 0.2% on a 100g delivered order pays 200mg (0.2g) of gold. Applies to every Maker company-wide. Changing this only affects orders delivered from now on — past commission entries keep the rate that was in effect when they were earned.
