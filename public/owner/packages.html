Use this when one customer order is actually several individual rings of different weights (e.g. 10 rings at 1.5g + the rest at 2.5g). Give them all the same Order Group code — each ring still has its own weight, karat, and delivery status.

Fine gold and net balance are calculated automatically from weight × karat and making charge. The estimated completion date is required and is used to track on-time delivery performance.
